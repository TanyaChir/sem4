## 5. Cross-cutting checks

- **Nationality.** Staff and intern rules differ by employer. OECD staff roles are generally limited to member-country (and some partner-country) nationals, and EU institutions require EU citizenship. I could not confirm how either applies to her, since I don't know her current citizenship. UN agency consultancies and the World Bank are generally more open. Check each posting's eligibility section first.
- **US work authorization.** US postdocs will need visa sponsorship (J-1 or H-1B) after her F-1/OPT period. Ask about it early.
- **Missing region.** The request read "the United States, , and European countries", with a gap. I treated Europe as including the UK, Switzerland and Norway. If another country was meant (for example Canada), I can search it.
